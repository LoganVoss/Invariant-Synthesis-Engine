# Industry applications

The reusable unit is not a sector-specific predictor. It is the collision-repair loop:
identify states that current observables collapse together, then discover a robust coordinate
that makes their consequential difference visible.

## Energy systems

- **Stability precursors:** recovering and growing oscillations overlap under standard event
  summaries; synthesize modal, phase, damping, or energy-flow coordinates.
- **Subsystem reduction:** test whether a high-dimensional region is behaviorally equivalent
  when represented by a small set of boundary coordinates.
- **Model inadequacy:** find residual relations that distinguish simulations that pass usual
  validation metrics but fail under real disturbances.
- **Adaptive observability:** use unresolved collision fibres to prioritize additional PMU,
  inverter, load, or interface measurements.
- **Cyberphysical consistency:** monitor joint dynamical relationships that must remain
  coherent even when individual sensor values appear plausible.

## Data centers and computing infrastructure

Large computing loads couple workload schedulers, power electronics, cooling, storage,
facility controls, and the grid. Consequential collisions can distinguish benign load changes
from synchronized or oscillatory regimes. Candidate coordinates can connect workload phase,
power ramp, cooling lag, and interface measurements while remaining stable across facility
size or server generation.

## Process and manufacturing systems

Safe process drift and runaway precursors often share pointwise ranges. The grammar can search
mass/energy-balance residues, response lags, boundary flows, derivative energies, and
scale-normalized ratios. Held-out plant/unit groups test whether a relation is transferable
or merely encodes one asset's calibration.

## Batteries and electrochemical fleets

Cells with different internal degradation states can produce similar voltage/current traces
under ordinary summaries. Invariant synthesis can search electrothermal relations that
separate future capacity loss while surviving charge-rate scaling, sensor offset, and device
groups—provided those transformations are physically justified.

## Aerospace, robotics, and autonomous systems

The relevant collision may be a recoverable perturbation versus incipient actuator or control
degradation. Frame changes, equivalent-actuator permutations, sign symmetries, and time shifts
can become explicit invariance contracts. The first deployment should remain offline or
advisory until extensive certification work is complete.

## Networks: cloud, telecom, and transportation

Ordinary congestion and cascade precursors can overlap at the node level. Graph-native
extensions can synthesize cut flows, propagation asymmetry, topology-normalized load, or
interface coordinates that transfer across network size and relabeling.

## Experimental science

Paired interventions and controlled transformations make the framework a symmetry-discovery
instrument. Even/odd responses around an operating point, scaling laws, time reversals,
permutations, and frame changes can expose coordinates in which a complicated phenomenon
becomes simpler. Any physical interpretation still requires independent derivation and
experiment.

## Product path

A credible adoption path is:

```text
offline discovery
→ retrospective engineering review
→ frozen prospective monitor
→ sensor/model design aid
→ tightly governed decision support
```

That path generates value without claiming autonomous control. The product is an engineering
object: an expression, its evidence, its failure cases, and the collision family it repairs.
